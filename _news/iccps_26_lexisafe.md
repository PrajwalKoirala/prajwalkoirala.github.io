**LexiSafe** received a **Best Paper Nomination** at **IEEE/ACM ICCPS 2026**. The work introduces a lexicographic prioritization framework for safe RL using partial policy freezing to reduce safety violations while maintaining strong performance in safety-critical CPSs, with an extension to multiple hierarchical safety objectives.
